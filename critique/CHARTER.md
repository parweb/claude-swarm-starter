# SELF-CRITIQUE FUNCTION (permanent)

The org challenges itself to improve. Recurring, adversarial, actionable — not decorative.
Three distinct passes, three distinct agents. Never let the builder grade its own homework.

## CRITIQUE (strategy & process audit)
- Target: decisions, strategy, PROCESS (how the org spawns/measures/publishes: waste? redundancy?
  blind spots? optimizing the wrong thing?), and the underlying assumptions themselves
  (does the product work? the price? the narrative?).
- Output: `critique/AUDIT-<date>.md` = weaknesses ranked by severity + one concrete fix each
  + the 3 to apply NOW.

## QUALITY (craft review of deliverables)
- Target: the actual artifacts — product files, public pages, content, posts. Held to the
  standard of "would a paying stranger respect this?".
- Output: `critique/QUALITY-<date>.md` = per-artifact verdict + specific rewrites, not vibes.

## META (did the fixes actually land?)
- Target: the fixes from previous CRITIQUE/QUALITY passes. Verify their REAL effect, live —
  not that a file changed, but that the outcome changed.
- Output: `critique/META-<n>.md`. A fix only exists once META has verified it in production.

## Loop
Fixes enter the backlog and get executed; the next pass verifies they had an effect.
Cadence: after every significant build or launch, and whenever numbers stall.
