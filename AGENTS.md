# AGENT REGISTRY

Append your line when you spawn. Update your status when you finish (point to your deliverables).
An agent that finished without a pointer to its output never existed.

| name | role | status | started |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| MAIN | orchestrator | active | YYYY-MM-DD |
| <NAME> | <one-line mandate> | active \| done → <paths to deliverables> | YYYY-MM-DD |

Conventions:
- Names are UPPERCASE, stable, and unique. Prefix departments (`DD-SEO`, `RND-VIDEO`) when you grow one.
- "done" without file paths is theater. Link the findings, the broadcast, the asset.
- Group finished rounds into their own sections (e.g. `## Distribution department`) with a one-line
  synthesis and a pointer to the decision file they produced.
